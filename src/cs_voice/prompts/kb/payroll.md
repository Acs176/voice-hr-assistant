# Payroll

## Pay schedule
Orbio pays every two weeks, on alternate Fridays. If a payday falls on a public
holiday, the deposit lands the business day before. Your first paycheck may cover
a partial period depending on your start date.

## Direct deposit
You set up direct deposit in the Orbio app under Profile, Pay, Bank details.
Changes take one full pay cycle to take effect, so a mid-cycle change applies to
the following paycheck, not the current one.

## Pay discrepancies
If a paycheck looks wrong, the most common causes are unapproved overtime, a
mid-cycle bank change, or a missed timesheet approval. HR can open a payroll
correction; corrections are paid out on the next regular payday, not immediately.

## Tax forms and payslips
Payslips are in the Orbio app under Pay, History. Your annual W-2 is posted by
January 31 and emailed to your personal address on file. Replacement W-2s for
prior years can be requested from HR.
