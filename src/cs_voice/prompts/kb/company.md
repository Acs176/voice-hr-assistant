# Company

## About Orbio
Orbio is an HR platform for frontline and shift-based workers. Employees here are
spread across several sites and are supported by a central People team.

## Office locations and hours
Orbio has offices in Austin, Denver, and a remote-first hub. Standard office hours
are 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. local time, Monday through Friday. Shift workers follow the
schedule in the Orbio app, not standard office hours.

## Contacting HR
The HR line is staffed 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays. For anything outside those
hours, leave a message in the Orbio app under Help, HR and someone responds within
one business day. Urgent safety issues should go to your site manager first.

## Holidays
Orbio observes 11 paid public holidays a year. The holiday calendar is in the
Orbio app under Schedule, Holidays. Holidays that fall on a weekend are observed
on the nearest weekday.
