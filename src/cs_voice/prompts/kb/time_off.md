# Time off

## PTO accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of paid time off per month, which is 20 days
a year. Accrual starts on your first day. Part-time employees accrue pro-rated to
their scheduled hours.

## Requesting time off
Submit time-off requests in the Orbio app under Schedule, Time off. Requests need
manager approval and should go in at least two weeks ahead for planned leave. You
can see your remaining balance on the same screen.

## Sick leave
Sick days are separate from PTO. Full-time employees get 8 paid sick days a year.
You do not need advance approval for sick leave, but you should notify your
manager before your shift starts. A doctor's note is required after three
consecutive sick days.

## Carry-over and payout
You can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year; anything
above that is forfeited on December 31. Unused PTO is paid out at your final rate
when you leave the company. Sick days are never paid out.
