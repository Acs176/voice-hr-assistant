# Onboarding

## First day
On day one, arrive at your assigned location for 9 a.m. and check in with
reception. Your manager will meet you for orientation. Remote hires get a video
orientation link by email the evening before.

## Required documents
Bring a government photo ID and your work-eligibility documents on your first day;
in the US that means completing the I-9. Upload your bank details for direct
deposit and your tax withholding form in the Orbio app within the first week.

## Equipment and accounts
Your laptop and Orbio account are provisioned before you start. If your login
isn't working on day one, contact IT, not HR. Your employee ID, in the format ORB
followed by four digits, is on your welcome email and your app profile.

## Probation period
New employees have a 90-day probation period. Benefits like the 401(k) match and
some leave types begin only after probation ends. Your manager reviews progress at
30, 60, and 90 days.
