# Benefits

## Health insurance
Orbio offers two medical plans, a standard PPO and a high-deductible plan with an
HSA. Coverage begins the first of the month after your start date. Orbio covers
80 percent of the employee premium and 50 percent for dependents.

## Adding dependents
You can add a spouse, domestic partner, or child during open enrollment or within
30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage or a birth. Add dependents in
the Orbio app under Benefits, then upload proof such as a marriage or birth
certificate.

## Retirement and 401(k)
Orbio matches 100 percent of the first 4 percent of salary you contribute to the
401(k). You are eligible after 90 days of employment. Matching contributions vest
immediately.

## Open enrollment
Open enrollment runs the first two weeks of November, with changes effective
January 1. Outside that window you can only change benefits after a qualifying
life event.
